# Scentique landing page: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation, not interviewed.** The owner said: "we want a super engaging and immersive but high converting landing page for Scentique, use our existing assets images etc and you can create stuff as well to match", "use our skills", and chose a luxurious brown glassy feel with parallax scroll. Evidence = their words and the assets in the repo; everything else below is an authored decision to be corrected. Surface mode (impeccable): **Persuade**. Mobile is a first-class target.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe.** Owner: "luxurious brown glassy", "premium", "engaging, immersive". Authored references: a candlelit apothecary, a Rembrandt chiaroscuro still life, the weight of a glass stopper in the hand.
2. **Journey.** Recognition (you meet the bottle) → Substance (what is inside it) → Range (four families, best sellers) → Proof (reviews, labelled placeholder) → Commitment (shop, quiz, letter).
3. **Energy curve.** Calm greet, one long rising push to the peak, a held plateau for the range, calm for proof, resolved close.
4. **Feeling curve and the one moment.** Below.
5. **One thing no site does.** *The scent develops as you scroll.* A perfume unfolds top, then heart, then base. Scroll is time, so a slim rail beside the hero lights the featured scent's real top, heart and base notes in turn as the camera pushes into the bottle.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** Dark editorial luxury: quiet type, one bold moment, restrained everything else.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** One unbroken world for the landing page (filmic). Shop, product, cart, checkout and account are ordinary pages.
8. **Assets.** 7 product photos, 4 family photos, 4 ingredient close-ups, brand SVGs, two Google Flow clips (desktop camera push-in 1280x720, phone smoke 720x1280). Hugging Face tests were rejected (low resolution, warped label).

## Grammar: Filmic one-shot, and why the other seven lost
A single linear argument with one emotional arc and a full-bleed scrub hero, which is the asset we own. Chaptered editorial bans the full-bleed scrub hero. Live surface needs a software product. Continuous world needs a chain of clips we do not have. Typographic poster bans photography. Gallery/catalog bans a single hero claim and copy over media, which is the hero. Split stage has no two-sided argument. Rhythmic cutlist bans pin and dwell and the brand is not high-energy.
Filmic bans (held): visible section counters or a progress readout, hard cuts between grounds, more than one entry point. Nav stays a fixed minimal bar. Conversion needs are met with one CTA per act, not an index.

## Feeling curve (one line per act: emotion, then what causes it)
1. **Hero push-in, 280svh (the peak).** Poise rising to awe: the camera falls toward the bottle through light and smoke until the monogram fills the screen, while the notes develop beside it.
2. **How it is made.** Calm curiosity: still photography, slow reveal, short real lines (blended by hand, rested, small runs).
3. **Range, four families and best sellers.** Choice: lateral pan across real product photos, prices visible, add to bag within reach.
4. **Proof.** Reassurance: quiet large quotes, the placeholder label kept.
5. **Close.** Resolved: one clear ask (shop), the letter, the quiz.

## The peak, the sentence, the silence
- **Peak:** the end of the hero push-in (act 1) with the base note lit. Visitor to a friend: "you scroll into a perfume bottle and the scent develops: top, heart, base."
- **It's the site where** the scent develops as you scroll.
- **Authored silence:** a short hold after the push-in ends, type-free, before the next act. Not dead scroll.

## Hero depth (skill standing preference)
Planes, back to front: the scrubbed clip (camera depth), a near atmospheric plane of drifting embers moving faster than the scroll, and the headline plane moving slower and fading before the peak so it never covers the bottle. Honest limit: the bottle is inside the video, not a separate cutout, so depth comes from the camera push plus those two planes.

## Design plan (frontend-design)
- **Colour (pinned by the owner, tokens only):** espresso #14100c, umber, cocoa, bone #efe6d6, champagne #c9a46a, family colours for dots.
- **Type (pinned):** Prata display, Bodoni Moda italic accent, Hanken Grotesk body. Kept the single italic phrase in the hero headline as a conscious brand exception.
- **Review against generic defaults, and what changed:** the draft used tracked all-caps labels on the CTA and chips, glass pills everywhere. Changed to sentence case, quieter chips (a colour dot and a word), and one primary action. No eyebrows, no counters, no card grid.
- **Layout, desktop:** copy left, scrub clip full bleed, development rail on the right edge. **Phone:** headline and one button at the bottom, the bottle and label clear above.

- **Principles:** spend the boldness on the push-in; everything else quiet; copy is the owner's existing copy plus clearly labelled placeholders; every motion has a reason.

## Score (device per act, none repeated twice in a row)
1 scrub + parallax (peak, 280svh) · 2 reveal · 3 pan · 4 drift · 5 pin + pointer (close).
